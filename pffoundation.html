    Years of Crafting Excellence

    Since 2010, Pure Finest Inc. has been redefining food and beverage standards, making a difference with premium products that inspire wellness, sustainability, and innovation. Explore our Story, Purpose, and Vision and more

    Who We Are
    • Overview We are focused on foods and beverages creating premium, plant-based, and natural foods and drinks. Offerings include REDEFINING REFRESHMENT WITH INTEGRITY AND INNOVATION vitality, vitamins, sports, drinks, and well-being foods made with organic ingredients to promote health and well-being. The company is known for using high-quality premium ingredients, alongside other carefully selected components designed to revitalize both body and soul. We ensure our brands are sustainable and beneficial for overall vitality, appealing to health-conscious consumers.
    • Origin Founded in the early 2010s, Pure Finest Inc.® has grown from a small startup into a globally recognized brand. The company began with a simple mission: to create beverages that are as beneficial to the body as they are delightful to the taste buds. Over the years, the PureFinest® brand has been dedicated to creating innovative and health-focused products that enhance the lives of consumers. With a commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction, PureFinest® continues to lead the way in developing food and beverages that not only taste great but also provide essential health benefits.
    • Our Drive Inspired by the holistic wellness traditions of the plant-based finest ingredients, PureFinest® Natural Vitamin, Sports, and Vitality Drinks were born from the vision of Beulah Group Inc. in early 2016. Driven by a passion for organic ingredients and cutting-edge nutritional science, BGI crafted a line of beverages that not only revitalize the body but also nourish the soul. Pre-launched in the heart of London, England, on March 15, 2023, PureFinest® redefined the functional drink market by introducing a range that enhances everyday health with superior, organically sourced ingredients. Committed to purity and performance, PureFinest® has ushered in a new era of wellness beverages, seamlessly blending ancient wisdom with modern vitality.
    • Autobiography PureFinest® and Taste Me!-Feel Me!® stand at the forefront of the food and beverage industry, under the umbrella of Pure Finest Inc.®, known for its unwavering commitment to innovation, quality, and sustainability. Since its inception, the company has been dedicated to creating products that meet consumers' needs and exceed their expectations 'Taste Me! Feel Me!®'. With a rich history of successful product launches, Pure Finest Inc.® has consistently pushed the boundaries of what is possible in the beverage and food industries.
    The PFI Way
    • Our Purpose Our mission is to enhance lives by offering high-quality food and beverages that inspire joy, promote well-being, and deliver satisfaction to the body and soul. We strive to be a reliable provider of innovative, wholesome products, thoughtfully created with care and excellence.
    • Our Vision A vision of a future where every bite and sip support sustainability, exceptional quality, and well-being. We aspire to create a world where our food and beverages not only satisfy but also foster healthier lifestyles and contribute to a more sustainable planet.
    • Our Approach With a commitment to quality and a focus on customer satisfaction, we blend premium ingredients, innovative technology, and eco-friendly methods to create food and beverages that excel in flavor and leave a lasting positive impact. This holistic approach ensures that everything we create not only delights the palate but also aligns with our values of wellness, sustainability, and innovation.

    PEOPLE AT THE HEART OF WHAT WE DO

    At Pure Finest Inc., we believe that people are our greatest asset. From empowering our employees to fostering meaningful connections with partners and supporting the communities we call home, we are committed to improving lives and creating a positive impact wherever we operate.

    EDI
    • Employee The Black, Asian, and Hispanic (BAH) Employee Networks celebrate and support their communities by promoting inclusion, equity, and cultural awareness. They offer learning forums, workshops, and events to boost personal and professional growth. Each network—BEN (Black), AIN (Asian), and HLIN (Hispanic/Latino)—empowers employees to embrace their identity, develop leadership, and help build a thriving, inclusive workplace.
    • Network We foster strong employment networks to empower and connect talent both within and beyond our organization. Our goal is to create a collaborative and inclusive community where individuals can access opportunities for professional growth, mentorship, and skill development. By nurturing meaningful relationships, we strive to build an environment that supports career advancement and enables everyone to reach their full potential. This network will strengthen employee engagement while aligning with Pure Finest Inc.’s brand ethos of innovation, sustainability, and wellness.
    • Headship At Pure Finest Inc., the leadership of our Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion (EDI) initiatives is dedicated to fostering a workplace where every individual feels valued, respected, and empowered to contribute their best. By embedding EDI as a core organizational value, the headship team is committed to creating a culture where belonging and inclusion thrive. Our vision is to establish Pure Finest Inc. as a benchmark for EDI, cultivating an environment where individuality is celebrated, opportunities are equitable, and innovation flourishes through diverse perspectives. By championing these principles, the leadership enables employees to bring their authentic selves to work and actively contribute to the company’s success, ensuring EDI is woven into every aspect of our operations and culture.
    Human Rights
    • Human Rights At Pure Finest Inc., we are firmly committed to upholding and promoting human rights across all aspects of our operations and value chain. Guided by international frameworks such as the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, we strive to ensure that respect for human rights is at the core of everything we do. This framework ensures that Pure Finest Inc. continues to lead responsibly, protecting and advancing human rights in every sphere of influence.
    • Governance The Pure Finest Inc. Board of Directors ensures that human rights policies are integrated into the company’s overall strategy and decision-making. A dedicated committee oversees the implementation and monitoring of human rights policies, engaging with stakeholders to address risks and opportunities. A robust policy framework governs our approach to human rights, including clear guidelines for due diligence, reporting, and remediation. We conduct regular human rights impact assessments across our operations and supply chains to identify and mitigate potential risks. All employees and partners receive training on human rights policies and practices to build a culture of awareness and accountability.
    • Stories of Impact Through its outreach programs, Pure Finest Inc. has positively impacted underserved communities by providing access to clean drinking water and nutrition education. For example, the company collaborated with grassroots organizations to fund water purification projects in regions facing scarcity, ensure clean water for thousands of families. Pure Finest Inc. has created mentorship programs that have directly led to a 30% increase in women taking up leadership roles within the company. These initiatives have not only empowered employees but have also inspired other organizations to adopt similar equity-driven models.

PURE FINEST FOUNDATION

The Pure Finest Foundation is deeply committed to creating a lasting positive impact in the communities it serves. Built on the pillars of sustainability, education, and community development, the foundation works to drive meaningful change through targeted initiatives and strategic partnerships. By collaborating with local and global organizations, the foundation supports programs that align with its core values, from advancing environmental sustainability to empowering underserved communities with access to education and resources. These efforts aim to uplift individuals, nurture growth, and promote equitable opportunities for all. Pure Finest Inc. believes that true progress stems from collective action. Through its partnerships with like-minded foundations, the organization seeks to empower individuals, foster innovation, and build a brighter, more inclusive future for generations to come.

PARTNERSHIPS

The Pure Finest Foundation partners with a diverse range of local and global organizations to amplify its mission of creating sustainable, equitable, and thriving communities. These partnerships are carefully aligned with the foundation's key focus areas: sustainability, education, and community development. Through these partnerships, the Pure Finest Inc. Foundation strives to empower individuals, address systemic challenges, and create a positive ripple effect that extends far beyond the communities it directly serves. By leveraging the expertise and passion of its partners, the foundation continues to advance its vision for a brighter, more sustainable future for all.

EQUITABILITY

Through these efforts, Pure Finest Inc. works tirelessly to create communities where every individual, regardless of their background or past challenges, has the opportunity to lead a fulfilling and dignified life. By addressing injustices and fostering collaboration, we aim to build a more equitable and hopeful future for all.
