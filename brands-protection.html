By buying counterfeit goods

  • you will not get the quality you are used to from PureFinest® Drinks, so at the end of the day you pay a higher price for the goods you are receiving.
  • you support criminals, and even terrorist groups, which finance their activities through the sale of counterfeit products, in addition to other methods.
  • you support the production of goods which may be made under inhumane conditions and inadequate health and environmental standards.
  • you increase your tax rate because the counterfeiters do not pay taxes like lawful companies, so the government will ultimately need to collect more taxes from you.

PureFinest® Drinks rigorously protects its absolute rights in its Brand and, as such, is interested in any background information regarding lookalike, copy-cat and/or counterfeit products.

If a deal is too good to be true, you are probably facing a counterfeit product. In case you find any such product on a website or an online marketplace, please email us on brandprotection@purefinestdrinks.com.

All you have to do here is to insert the link to the suspicious product and we will automatically investigate and remove it from the online marketplace, if possible.

Furthermore, we kindly ask you to insert your email address so that we can contact you in case any further information is needed.
